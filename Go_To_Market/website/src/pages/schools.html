For K–2 teachers and school leaders

A ten-minute writing center that runs itself and leaves something for the portfolio.

A child says what happened today, the words appear on a ruled page, and they write over them in their own hand. The app marks each letter as they write, teaches stroke order when a letter is drawn out of order, and keeps every finished page as a dated journal in the child's handwriting.

Download the one-page PDF   Ask about a classroom pilot

Runs itself

No logins, no Wi-Fi, no class setup. A child picks their name and taps New Entry. Stopping part-way is fine — the words wait on the page for next time.

Shared iPads work

Several children on one iPad, a profile each, with an optional PIN. Each keeps their own journal, typeface and size.

Something for the portfolio

Any page, a month, or everything exports as a PDF, with the typed words alongside if you want them, for the folder or the parent conference.

In the classroom

  1. Set up in five minutes. Install from Apps and Books. Add a profile per child on each iPad and pick a letter style and size for the class. No accounts, nothing to configure.
  2. Center time. Ten minutes at a station: say it, write it, tap I'm finished. The practice sheet — every letter Aa to Zz and 0 to 9, each drawing itself stroke by stroke — is the warm-up.
  3. Conference time. Export a child's journal as a PDF. Progress is kept per font-and-size setting, so a harder setting never looks like regression.

For the office

  • The children's work stays on the iPad. No account, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or server holds a child's writing. Speech recognition runs on the iPad and no audio is recorded. Nothing a child writes leaves the device unless an adult exports a PDF. Privacy policy.
  • Cost. The basic app is always free. Distribute it through Apps and Books in Apple School Manager like any other app — nothing to license, no per-seat cost, no subscription. Some additional features may be paid in future; there are none in this version.
  • Paperwork. A one-page data statement is ready for district student-data forms; it confirms that no student work, identity, device, analytics, or crash-reporting data is sent. Ask for it.
  • Setup. iPad, iOS 18 or later, and Apple Pencil is recommended for each iPad. Left-handed layout, color-blind ink and Dynamic Type are built in.

Free classroom pilot — autumn 2026

Five to ten K–2 classrooms, October to January. Install it on the classroom iPads, use it as a center or station for a few weeks, and fill in one short form at the end. Your feedback sets what is built next for classrooms, and you hear about it first. Email to take part.

What it does not have yet

No teacher dashboard, no class roster, no sync between iPads. iPad only — no Chromebook or Android. Print letters only, English only. We would rather you knew before you said yes.
