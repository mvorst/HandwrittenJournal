Privacy policy

Last updated {{UPDATED}}

Your child's journal never leaves the iPad. The words, the writing, the photos and the scores stay on the device; there is no account and no server that holds them. The app sends anonymous crash reports and usage statistics so we can fix problems and improve it. That data contains no journal content, no names and no contact details, is never used for advertising, and we will never sell it.

This website uses Google Analytics to see which pages help; details are in section 10.

1. What stays on your iPad

The app keeps the following on the device it is installed on, and does not send it anywhere:

  • Profiles — a name, an optional photo, an optional four-digit PIN (stored only as a salted hash, never as the digits) and writing settings such as the typeface and size.
  • Journal entries — the words your child spoke or typed, the pen strokes they wrote, accuracy scores, points, and the date and time of each entry.
  • Practice — which letters were traced on the practice sheet on which day, and the points that earned.

All of it is written to storage on your iPad and protected by iOS file protection. The only way any of it leaves the device is when an adult exports a PDF and shares it (section 6).

2. What the app collects

To find and fix problems and to see which features are used, the app sends two kinds of anonymous information:

  • Crash reports — what the app was doing when it crashed or froze (the screen and the sequence of technical steps), the app version, the iPad model and iOS version, free memory, and the time. A crash report never includes the words on the page, the writing, a name or a photo.
  • Usage statistics — which screens and features are used and how often (for example, that an entry was dictated rather than typed, that the practice sheet was opened, or that a typeface was changed), how long a writing session lasted, the app version, iPad model and iOS version, and the language and region setting. Never the content of an entry, never a child's name, never a photo, never precise location.

These are tied to a random identifier generated on the device that is not linked to your Apple ID, your email address or any other information about you or your child. We use this data only to operate and improve the app — never for advertising, never to build a profile of a child, and never to sell or rent to anyone.

3. Who processes it

Crash reports and usage statistics are sent to {{ANALYTICS_PROVIDER}}, which processes them on our behalf under an agreement that prohibits any other use. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPad's Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple also provides us with aggregated crash and usage information through App Store Connect; Apple's own privacy policy governs that. We keep crash and usage data for no longer than {{ANALYTICS_RETENTION}} and then delete it.

4. Children

Handwritten Journal is designed for children, used with a grown-up. The app has no account to create, no sign-in, no way to contact anyone from inside it, and no advertising. The anonymous crash and usage data described in section 2 is collected solely to support the app's internal operations — fixing bugs and improving features — and is never used to contact a child, to track a child across other apps or websites, or for behavioral advertising. We do not collect a child's name, photo, voice, writing or any other personal information.

5. Speech recognition

Speech is converted to text using Apple's on-device speech recognition. The app requests on-device recognition specifically, so the audio is not sent to Apple's servers for this purpose. The app does not record audio and keeps no audio files: the microphone feeds the recognizer and nothing else.

7. Deleting

Deleting an entry deletes its writing and its scores. Deleting a profile deletes everything belonging to that profile. Deleting the app removes all of it from your iPad. Crash and usage data cannot be linked back to you, so there is nothing for us to find or erase on request; it expires on the schedule in section 3.

8. We will never sell your data

The basic app is always free, because we want children to thrive. If we introduce additional features, they may be paid — because we will never sell your data, show advertising, or share what the app collects with anyone for their own purposes.

9. Purchases

This version of the app has no in-app purchases. If paid features are offered in a future version, Apple would process the payment through the App Store; we would receive no personal information about you beyond what Apple's purchase reporting provides. This policy would be updated before any such change.

10. This website

This website is a set of static pages with no forms. It uses Google Analytics (GA4) to measure which pages are visited, roughly where visitors come from and how they move through the site. Google Analytics sets cookies on your browser (such as _ga) to tell returning visits from new ones, and processes the data on Google's servers; Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. We use the reports only to improve the site. We do not use Google Analytics for advertising and have not enabled its advertising features.

You can opt out by blocking cookies for this site in your browser, by installing Google's browser add-on, or by turning on the Global Privacy Control signal in your browser — when it is set, this site does not load Google Analytics at all. Google's own privacy policy describes how Google handles the data.

The company that hosts the site may keep standard server logs (such as the requesting address, the time and the page requested) for security and reliability, as any web host does. If you email us, we keep your message for as long as we need it to reply and to keep a record of the conversation.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app or the website changes in a way that affects this policy, the policy will be updated here, with the date at the top, before that version is released.
