Privacy policy

Last updated {{UPDATED}}

Your child's journal stays on the iPad. The app has no account, advertising, third-party analytics, or crash-reporting software. It does not transmit journal data, names, photos, speech, or device information to us or another company.

1. What stays on your iPad

The app keeps the following on the device it is installed on and does not send it anywhere:

  • Profiles — a name, an optional photo, an optional four-digit PIN (stored only as a salted hash, never as the digits), and writing settings such as the typeface and size.
  • Journal entries — the words your child spoke or typed, the pen strokes they wrote, accuracy scores, points, and the date and time of each entry.
  • Practice — which letters were traced on the practice sheet on which day, and the points that earned.

The app writes this information to storage on your iPad. The only way it leaves the device is when a grown-up exports a PDF and shares it (section 4).

2. What the app does not collect

Handwritten Journal does not collect or transmit personal information, device information, analytics, or crash reports. There is no sign-in and no server that holds a child's journal.

3. Children

Handwritten Journal is designed for children and used with a grown-up. We do not collect a child's name, photo, voice, writing, location, persistent identifier, or other personal information. The app has no advertising and no way for a child to contact anyone from inside it.

4. Speech, permissions, and sharing

Speech is converted to text using Apple's on-device speech recognition. The app does not record audio or keep audio files: the microphone feeds the recognizer and nothing else.

The app asks for permissions only when a feature needs them, explains why first, and works without each of them:

PermissionUsed forIf you decline
Microphone and speech recognitionTurning what your child says into words to traceThe words can be typed instead

When a grown-up exports a page or the journal as a PDF and shares it through the system share sheet, what happens to that file afterwards is governed by whichever app or service they choose.

5. Deleting

Deleting an entry deletes its writing and scores. Deleting a profile deletes everything belonging to that profile. Deleting the app removes all of it from your iPad.

6. We will never sell your data

The basic app is always free, because we want children to thrive. If we introduce additional features, they may be paid — because we will never sell your data, show advertising, or share what the app collects with anyone for their own purposes.

7. Purchases

This version of the app has no in-app purchases. If paid features are offered in a future version, Apple would process the payment through the App Store; we would receive no personal information about you beyond what Apple's purchase reporting provides. This policy would be updated before any such change.

8. This website

This website is a set of static pages with no forms, analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking scripts. The company that hosts the site may keep standard server logs, such as the requesting address, the time, and the page requested, for security and reliability. If you email us, we keep your message for as long as we need it to reply and keep a record of the conversation.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app or the website changes in a way that affects this policy, the policy will be updated here with the date at the top before that version is released.
